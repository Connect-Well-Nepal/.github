# Connect Well Nepal 🇳🇵

**Bridging the gap in mental and digital healthcare access across Nepal.**

## 📖 Project Overview
**Connect Well Nepal** is a cross-platform mobile application designed to provide equitable, confidential, and quality digital healthcare. Our mission is to support youth mental health and connect rural populations with professional medical care through technology.

### Key Features (Planned)
- **Telehealth:** Video & chat consultations with mental health professionals.
- **Clinic Finder:** Locate nearby hospitals and clinics with a map view.
- **Education Hub:** Access to articles and resources on mental wellness.
- **Symptom Checker:** AI-powered preliminary health assessment.
- **Offline Mode:** Critical features work without active internet.

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack
* **Framework:** [Flutter](https://flutter.dev/) (Dart) - *One codebase for Android & iOS*
* **Backend:** [Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/) (Auth, Firestore, Storage)
* **Video Calls:** [Agora.io](https://www.agora.io/)
* **State Management:** `setState` (Moving to Provider/Riverpod later)
* **Architecture:** MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel)
